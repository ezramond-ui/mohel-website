# How to edit your website's words

You can change any wording yourself, right in your browser on GitHub — no
coding and nothing to install. When you save ("commit"), the live site
updates on its own in about 1–2 minutes.

There are two ways. Use whichever is comfortable.

---

## Way 1 — Edit one page (simplest)

1. Open the page you want to change using its link in the table below.
   (You'll be signed into GitHub already.)
2. GitHub shows the page's text with an **edit view**. If you see a pencil
   icon (✏️) in the top-right, click it first.
3. Press **Ctrl + F** (Windows) or **Cmd + F** (Mac) and type a few words
   exactly as they appear on your site — e.g. `our unbroken mesorah`.
   It jumps right to them.
4. Type your changes.
5. Scroll to the bottom (or click the green **Commit changes…** button),
   then confirm **Commit changes**.
6. Wait 1–2 minutes and refresh your site.

## Way 2 — Edit everything at once (search the whole site)

Good when the same phrase appears on several pages.

1. Go to **https://github.dev/ezramond-ui/mohel-website**
   (or open the repo on github.com and press the **`.`** key).
   This opens a full editor in your browser.
2. Press **Ctrl + Shift + F** (Cmd + Shift + F on Mac) to search **all**
   files at once. Type the words you want to change.
3. Edit them wherever they appear.
4. Click the **Source Control** icon on the left (branch icon), type a
   short message, and click **Commit & Push** (the ✓).
5. The site updates in 1–2 minutes.

---

## The golden rules

- **Only change the words**, i.e. the text you can read. Don't change
  anything inside the angle brackets `< … >` — those are the building
  blocks that make the page work.
- **Saving = publishing.** Every commit goes live. There's no separate
  "publish" button, and every version is saved, so nothing is ever lost.
- **The menu bar and footer repeat on every page.** If you change one of
  those, it only changes on that one page. For those, use Way 2, or just
  ask me and I'll do it everywhere at once.
- **Leave these to me** (they appear in two places and must stay in sync):
  - **FAQ answers** — each answer is also stored in a hidden block for
    Google. I'll keep both matched.
  - **Your name, phone number, or the "Certified Expert Mohel" title.**
  - Anything you want changed **sitewide**.

When in doubt, change it and look — if it doesn't come out right, tell me
the page and I'll fix it in seconds.
